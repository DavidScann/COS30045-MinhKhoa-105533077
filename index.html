Televisions: The Energy Story

Television technology has evolved rapidly, bringing bigger screens and sharper images to our living rooms. But what does this mean for our energy bills and the environment? Explore the data below to understand the current landscape of the Australian TV market.

1. The Landscape of Choice

When shopping for a new TV, Australian consumers are presented with a variety of display technologies. Standard LED/LCD screens still dominate portions of the market due to affordability, but modern tech like OLED is prominent.

The market is also highly competitive, with numerous brands vying for attention. Some brands offer a massive range of models, giving consumers plenty of choices but making decision-making complex.

2. Bigger is Better?

There is a clear trend towards larger screens. The data shows a significant concentration of available models in the 50-70 inch range. While a cinematic experience is desirable, it's important to consider that larger screen real estate often requires more power.
